The system

  1. One object. Each picture is a single thing, floating over its own soft shadow on a plain backdrop.
  2. Made of layers. It is built from a few rounded slabs, stacked or fanned, so the layers are what is read.
  3. A fixed kit of materials. Gradient gloss, stitched leather, frosted glass and crystal, holographic foil, and matte metals (brushed, plain and gold), speckled stone and one lit layer.
  4. One palette. A main pair of colours, a contrasting second pair, one accent, one backdrop. Every icon takes any palette.
  5. One studio. The same camera and soft light, with coloured lamps seen only in reflections, which is where glass and metal get their colour.
  6. Made details. Stitches, printed lines, a card's chip, screws, and one or two small loose pieces for life.
